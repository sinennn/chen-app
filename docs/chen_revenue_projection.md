## Revenue Streams

### 1. Freemium Subscriptions (Primary)
- Price: **₦900/month** via Paystack

- Premium: Unlimited Chen, all themes, voice notes, detailed compatibility

### 2. Artist & Label Partnerships (Month 6+)

- Estimated **₦150,000 per event**
- Target: 1 event/month from Month 6

### 3. Premium Theme Drops (Future)
- Limited edition themes tied to cultural moments
- Detty December, AFCON season, artist collabs

## Reality Check

- These projections assume consistent marketing and a working app. Neither is guaranteed.
- The 5% free-to-premium conversion is achievable but requires a genuinely good product.
- Churn (people cancelling) is your biggest enemy. Keep it low by shipping new features regularly.
- ₦900/month feels small but 1,000 users = ₦900,000/month. The math works — you just need users.
- Your real goal in Year 1 is not revenue. It is learning what makes users stay.
- ElevenLabs voice for Chen will cost money at scale — factor this in before enabling it for free users.
